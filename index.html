Method and limits

Deterministic, case-insensitive JavaScript matches complete skill names and explicit aliases. A recognized mention is not necessarily a mandatory requirement: negation, proficiency, years of experience, importance and context are not inferred. Resume evidence is quoted text from confirmed entries; a mention does not prove proficiency. This is not an ATS score or a guarantee of hiring outcomes.
